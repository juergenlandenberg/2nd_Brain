This is the derivatives knowledge map. It deliberately separates instrument knowledge from strategy knowledge. No strategy is treated as approved merely because it appears in the source vault.

## Futures knowledge

### Instrument fundamentals

- Contract size, tick size and tick value
- Initial and maintenance margin
- Mark-to-market and daily settlement
- Expiry, rollover and delivery
- Contango, backwardation and basis
- Liquidity, slippage and trading hours

### Futures by underlying

- Equity-index futures
- Interest-rate futures
- Commodity futures
- Energy futures
- Metals futures
- Agricultural futures
- Currency futures
- Crypto futures

### Futures strategy families

- Directional trend following
- Momentum and breakout systems
- Mean reversion
- Calendar spreads
- Inter-commodity spreads
- Basis and cash-and-carry
- Hedging an existing exposure
- Volatility and event hedging

## Options knowledge

### Instrument fundamentals

- Calls and puts
- Strike, expiry and moneyness
- Intrinsic and extrinsic value
- Implied versus realized volatility
- Greeks: delta, gamma, theta, vega and rho
- Exercise, assignment and settlement
- Margin and liquidity risk

### Options strategy families

#### Defined-risk directional

- Long call
- Long put
- Bull call spread
- Bear put spread
- Bull put spread
- Bear call spread

#### Income and carry

- Covered call
- Cash-secured put
- Credit spreads
- Iron condor
- Iron butterfly

#### Volatility and event structures

- Straddle
- Strangle
- Calendar spread
- Diagonal spread
- Ratio spread
- Long-volatility and short-volatility structures

#### Protection and portfolio overlays

- Protective put
- Collar
- Put spread hedge
- Delta hedge
- Tail-risk hedge

## Strategy review template

For each concrete strategy, create a separate note only when source material is substantive. Record:

- Market thesis and invalidation condition
- Payoff diagram or payoff description
- Defined and undefined risks
- Greeks and sensitivity to time/volatility
- Margin and liquidity requirements
- Entry, management and exit rules
- Scenario table: favorable, neutral and adverse
- Fees, financing and tax questions
- Backtest or live-evidence status
- Source, date and reviewer

## Current status

The current import confirms Futures and Options as source taxonomy areas. Concrete strategy notes must be extracted from substantive `xx_Main` material; this page does not invent or endorse strategies.

# Zebra ZQ220 Plus

Portable Zebra receipt/label printer reference.

## Key specifications

- Thermal direct printing; continuous receipt paper, reflective-mark media, and labels with gaps.
- Media widths: 80 mm; with spacers 76.2 mm, 58 mm, and 50.8 mm.
- Roll core: 12.7 mm; maximum roll diameter: 50 mm.
- Media thickness: 0.058–0.1575 mm.
- Maximum print width: 72 mm; resolution 203 dpi; maximum speed 50 mm/s.
- Battery: Li-ion, 2500 mAh, 7.4 V.
- Interfaces: Bluetooth 5.0 LE dual mode, USB-C, passive NFC tag.
- Languages and tools include CPCL, a subset of ESC/POS, Zebra setup utilities, and Zebra Designer.

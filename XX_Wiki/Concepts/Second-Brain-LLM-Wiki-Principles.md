## Preserved principles

- Separate immutable RAW sources from curated WIKI knowledge.
- Maintain a central index and explicit graph links.
- Process sources deliberately instead of repeatedly rediscovering the same information.
- Preserve provenance and keep original material available for verification.
- Use routing and targeted search to reduce unnecessary context loading.
- Treat summaries as lossy transformations that require review.

## Compatibility with current 2ndB policy

The RAW/WIKI separation, provenance, indexing and graph principles are compatible. The source's suggestions around QMD, automatic RAW-to-WIKI publishing, autonomous agent writing and unrestricted automation are not automatically adopted. Current 2ndB governance remains authoritative.

### **The Trading Process: Step-by-Step Strategy**

The core execution strategy follows a strict sequential funnel starting from macro direction down to micro execution:

#### **Step 1: Macro Direction (4-Hour Chart)**

- **Objective:** Identify the overarching, dominant market trend and structural position within the current Elliott Wave cycle.
    
- **Action:** Establish whether the broader market sentiment is within an impulsive (trend) phase or a corrective phase. Define the primary directional bias (Long or Short).

#### **Step 2: Structural Confirmation & Target Setting (1-Hour Chart)**

- **Objective:** Map internal sub-wave structures and identify key logical pivot zones.
    
- **Action:** Validate the internal wave subdivisions to ensure they support the 4-hour macro thesis. Define critical target projection levels (using Fibonacci extensions/retracements) and map major horizontal support and resistance zones.

### **Boundary Conditions and Constraints**

To protect capital and prevent emotional over-trading, the Kompendium enforces strict parameters:

- **The Invalidation Constraint (Make-or-Break / MOB Lines):** Every structure has a definitive mathematical line in the sand. For instance, according to Elliott Wave rules, _Wave 2 can never retrace more than 100% of Wave 1_. If price action breaches the specific MOB level established on the 4h/1h chart, the entire counting scenario is immediately invalidated, and the trade plan must be discarded.
    
- **The Dual-Confirmation Rule:** A user must never execute a trade based solely on a standalone signal (such as an automated arrow or an isolated indicator cross on a lower timeframe). The strategy requires a top-down confirmation pipeline—written/structural macro analysis **must** align with real-time livestream technical triggers before entry.
    
- **The CRV Threshold:** Trades must exhibit asymmetric risk-reward profiles. Entry timing on the 5-minute chart is constrained to setups where the potential profit heavily outweighs the risked capital (often targeting high Risk-Reward Ratios up to 10:1 or more).
    
- **Side-line Constraint in Chaotic Markets:** When the 15-minute or 1-hour charts signal a highly erratic or sideways consolidation environment that lacks clear impulsive characteristics, the strategy mandates non-action ("Not trading is better trading").
    
- **Risk/Capital Preservation Constraints:** Capital preservation is prioritized strictly over profit generation. The framework expects a learning and practice window of 6–12 months before a trader achieves consistent profitability.

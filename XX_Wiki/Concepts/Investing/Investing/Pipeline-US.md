Pipeline US 2026-07-18

Status: 
Category: Investment ideas
Geo: mainly US Canada

Companies that profit from oil and gas pipeline projects are generally categorized into **midstream energy companies**, specialized **engineering and construction (E&C) firms**, and **components manufacturers**.

Because pipelines function like "toll booths"—charging fees based on the volume of product moved rather than the fluctuating price of the commodity itself—these companies generate highly consistent cash flows.

### 1. Midstream Operators (The Owners and Managers)

These massive energy infrastructure giants own, expand, and operate the actual pipeline networks. They profit directly from long-term transportation contracts.

- **Enbridge Inc. (ENB):** Operates the world’s longest and most complex crude oil and liquids transportation system, moving roughly 30% of all crude produced in North America.
    
- **Kinder Morgan (KMI):** Owns North America's largest natural gas transmission network (spanning roughly 66,000 miles of pipelines), moving approximately 40% of U.S. natural gas volume.
    
- **Energy Transfer LP (ET):** A highly diversified master limited partnership (MLP) operating more than 130,000 miles of pipelines across the U.S. for crude oil, natural gas, NGLs, and refined products.
    
- **The Williams Companies (WMB):** A heavy hitter in natural gas, handling nearly one-third of all natural gas used daily in the United States via systems like the Transco pipeline.
    
- **Enterprise Products Partners (EPD):** A premier integrated midstream company operating over 50,000 miles of pipelines focused heavily on Natural Gas Liquids (NGLs), crude, and petrochemical logistics.
    
- **TC Energy (TRP):** A Canadian giant commanding vast networks across Canada, the U.S., and Mexico, critical to both domestic distribution and LNG export feeding.
    
- **ONEOK (OKE) & MPLX (MPLX):** Major integrated operators with expansive regional footprints connecting shale plays (like the Permian and Bakken) to processing and export hubs.
    

### 2. Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) Firms

Before a midstream company can profit from a pipeline, these specialized international engineering firms are paid billions to design, excavate, and lay the infrastructure.

- **Quanta Services (PWR):** A dominant contractor in North America providing infrastructure solutions, trenching, and pipeline construction services.
    
- **Fluor Corporation (FLR) & Jacobs (J):** Global heavy-civil engineering firms that manage the complex project management, procurement, and mega-construction operations for transcontinental lines and processing facilities.
    
- **MasTec (MTZ):** Specialized in large-scale infrastructure construction, managing extensive pipeline installations, compressor stations, and joint-venture energy builds.
    

### 3. Equipment & Technology Providers

Pipelines require constant monitoring, high-powered compression, safety valves, and specialized materials. These firms profit by selling the physical components and tech layers.

- **Caterpillar Inc. (CAT):** Supplies the heavy machinery (pipelayers, excavators) necessary to physically clear routes and drop pipe into the ground globally.
    
- **Baker Hughes (BKR):** Provides high-tech turbomachinery, compression systems (essential for keeping gas moving through pipelines), and pipeline integrity testing technologies.
    
- **Emerson Electric (EMR):** Specializes in automated valves, regulators, and digital software systems used to monitor pressures and prevent leaks along thousands of miles of pipe.

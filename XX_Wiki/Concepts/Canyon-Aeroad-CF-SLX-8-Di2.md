## Component dimensions

- Spacer: 20 mm across sizes.
- Crank length: 165 mm for 2XS; 170 mm for XS; 172.5 mm for S through XL; 175 mm for 2XL.
- Chainring: 52/36 across sizes.
- Seatpost length: 275 mm for 2XS; 301 mm for XS through 2XL.
- Maximum seatpost extension: 166 mm.
- Minimum seatpost insertion: 66 mm.
- Tyre size: 28 inches.
- Brake disc dimensions shown as 140/140 for 2XS and XS, 160/140 for S through XL, and 160/160 for 2XL.

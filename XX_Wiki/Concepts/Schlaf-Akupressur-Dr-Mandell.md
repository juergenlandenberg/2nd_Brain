# Schlaf-Akupressur nach Dr. Mandell

![[Medical-acupunture_sleep_hand.png]]

The image above is the preserved source attachment. The text below is an OCR-assisted and source-checked summary; the anatomical labels remain approximate.

## Described procedure

The source describes holding each point for approximately **20–30 seconds**, with firm but tolerable pressure, and repeating on the other hand if desired.

The illustrated points are described as:

1. A point below the second digit, near the relevant metacarpal/hand crease.
2. A tender band in the thenar eminence, presented by the source as a breathing-related point.
3. A point along the lower line of the fifth digit, described in the source as related to the heart meridian.
4. A hollow just inside the pisiform bone at wrist level.

The source attributes effects such as parasympathetic activation, reduced anxiety, slower respiration, improved sleep, and improved oxygen intake to the technique. These are **claims made by the source**, not verified conclusions in this note.

## Safety and evidence status

- This note is a source summary, not medical advice.
- Acupressure point identification from the screenshot is approximate; anatomical placement should not be inferred with clinical precision.
- The cited systematic reviews are preserved as source references but have not been independently appraised in this import.
- Stop if there is pain, numbness, dizziness, or other adverse reaction; consult a qualified professional for persistent sleep problems or medical conditions.

# Source Policy

## Trust order
1. Direct primary source or official data.
2. Original paper, transcript or document.
3. Reputable secondary analysis with cited evidence.
4. Search snippets, summaries and commentary — discovery only, not final evidence.

## Research intake
Each source should record URL or file path, title, author/publisher, publication date when known, retrieval date, source type, claims extracted, limitations and related WIKI pages.

## Conflicts
Conflicting claims are not merged by averaging or guessing. Keep both claims, identify the source and date, explain the disagreement and mark the issue for review.
